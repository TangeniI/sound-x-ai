# Sample credits

Every sound in this folder comes from a real recording. Nothing is synthesised.
`scripts/build_samples.py` trims, fades and normalises the originals.

| Files | Source | Licence |
|---|---|---|
| `kick_909*`, `clap_909`, `rim_909`, `hat_909_*`, `ride_909`, `crash_909` | Roland TR-8 hardware, recorded by Mck in [MckSamplePacks](https://github.com/MckAudio/MckSamplePacks) | CC0 1.0 |
| `kick_808_long`, `clap_808`, `rim_808`, `hat_808_*`, `cymbal_808_long`, `cowbell_808`, `tom_808` | Roland TR-808 recordings by Michael Fischer, 1994, via [sounds-tr808-fischer](https://github.com/tidalcycles/sounds-tr808-fischer) | CC0 1.0 |
| `perc_tap`, `perc_blip`, `chord_high`, `pad_*`, `fx_bed` | Donated by [The Black Dog](https://theblackdogma.com) to [Sonic Pi](https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi) | CC0 1.0 |
| `kick_tek`, `kick_klub`, `hat_raw`, `bass_*`, `chord_em9`, `impact`, `boom`, `vinyl`, `drone` | [Freesound](https://freesound.org) contributors, via the [Sonic Pi sample library](https://github.com/sonic-pi-net/sonic-pi/blob/main/etc/samples/README.md) | CC0 1.0 |
| `anvil`, `brake_drum`, `clap_hands`, `shaker`, `gong`, `gong_scrape` | [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) | CC0 1.0 |
| `fm_*`, `clavi_*` | Yamaha TX81Z hardware, from the [Versilian Community Sample Library](https://github.com/sgossner/VCSL) | CC0 1.0 |
| `ir_hall` | Impulse response of the Divinity School, Oxford, from [House of Commons - Acoustic Reconstruction 1820-34](https://zenodo.org/record/7560885), University of York AudioLab. Trimmed and decoded to stereo. | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |
| `ir_room` | Impulse responses of the York Guildhall Council Chamber, from the same dataset. Trimmed, two positions combined as stereo. | [CC BY 4.0](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/4.0/) |

The 909-labelled sounds come from Roland's TR-8, which recreates the TR-909 digitally.
The 808-labelled sounds in the second row are the original analogue machine.
