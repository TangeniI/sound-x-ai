Free techno grooves for your DAW

Sound × AI writes dark, hypnotic techno in your browser, from twelve hand-written groove families played on recordings of real drum machines (TR-808, TR-909), metal and rooms. Change it while it plays, then take it into Ableton Live, FL Studio, Logic, Bitwig or any DAW. It's free, there's no signup, and nothing to install.

What you can take away

  • Loops of 4, 8 or 16 bars as WAV that repeat without a click
  • Seven stems in one zip, the kick on its own
  • MIDI of every part: drums on General MIDI notes, bass, chords, sequence
  • An Ableton Live 12 set: the stems, every part as MIDI and the sections marked, in one project
  • A DJ version: about seven minutes, 32 bars of drums at each end to mix in and out
  • DJ tools: a drum loop, the same without the kick, and the build into the drop
  • WAV or AIFF, 24 or 16-bit, tagged with BPM, key and Camelot number
  • MIDI out with clock, so your DAW or drum machine plays along

Questions

Is it free?
Yes. No account, no watermark, and the sounds are CC0 or CC BY.
Is it an AI song generator?
No. The Groove style is written from hand-built rules and recorded instruments, not trained on anyone's tracks. The other styles use a small neural network for one line. It gives you a starting point; you finish the track.
Can I release what I make?
It claims no rights in your exports. Stores now refuse tracks made wholly by generators, so make it your own first. This isn't legal advice.
What does it know about me?
Nothing personal. It counts visits and which buttons get used (plays, exports, shares) with GoatCounter: no cookies, no tracking across sites. Your sounds, settings and anything you make stay in your browser.
Which browsers?
Any current browser plays and exports. MIDI out needs Chrome, Edge or Firefox.
